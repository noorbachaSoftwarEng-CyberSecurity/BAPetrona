# BAPetrona – Oil Sales & Purchase Management System

BAPetrona is a full-stack business management system developed for managing oil sales, purchases, inventory, invoices, payments, expenses, income tracking, and reporting.  
The system is designed for businesses dealing with vehicle oils, lubricants, engine oils, and related petroleum products.

This project provides a modern and efficient solution for handling daily business operations with advanced filtering, searching, reporting, and invoice management features.

---

## 🚀 Features

### 🔹 Party Management
- Add, update, delete, and manage parties/customers/suppliers
- Store customer contact and business details
- Search and filter party records

### 🔹 Item & Category Management
- Manage oil products and inventory items
- Item categorization support
- Stock quantity tracking
- Unit type management

### 🔹 Sales Management
- Create sale invoices
- Track customer payments
- Partial and full payment support
- Remaining balance calculations
- Sale return invoice system

### 🔹 Purchase Management
- Create purchase invoices
- Supplier payment tracking
- Purchase return invoice system
- Expense integration

### 🔹 Invoice System
- Professional invoice generation
- Printable invoices
- Payment status tracking
- Return invoice support

### 🔹 Expense & Income Tracking
- Manage overall expenses
- Manage income records
- Categorized expense and income handling

### 🔹 Reports & Analytics
- Generate reports between selected dates
- Sales reports
- Purchase reports
- Expense reports
- Income reports
- Financial summaries

### 🔹 Advanced Data Features
- Server-side pagination
- Dynamic searching
- Filtering and sorting
- Responsive data tables
- Real-time updates

### 🔹 Authentication & Security
- User authentication
- Protected routes
- Secure API handling

## 🎯 Purpose of the Project

The purpose of BAPetrona is to simplify and digitize the management process for oil trading businesses by providing:

- Accurate invoice handling
- Inventory tracking
- Financial management
- Payment monitoring
- Detailed business reporting

The system can be adapted for:
- Engine oil businesses
- Lubricant stores
- Petroleum product suppliers
- Automotive oil distributors
